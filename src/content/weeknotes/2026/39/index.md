# Where did Unity Game Engine come from ?

Unity came into the picture because [Ananya](https://github.com/AnanyaBottu) decided 
to use it and now we must stick with it. I have been looking into the other 
engines as well and it seems like a good choice currently. While `godot` and 
`bevy` seem simpler, Unity has significantly more tutorials and is better 
built for different form-factors (especially the mobile). With `bevy`, the whole 
troupe of writing Rust is a major hiccup as it'll get in the way of development.

So, Unity it is.

Interestingly, this has lead to my entire youtube recommendation realigning 
itself into game development only and now I feel like I am not doing enough! 
There is a lot to learn and from what I am told, a good deal of mathematics will 
come into picture. Frankly speaking, I am looking forward to it.

[Visruth](https://github.com/beppvis) has been kind enough to help me out with 
different resources, hot takes, asset stores etc. I can say with certainty that 
this has sped up my development but I can go one step further.

Earlier it was not obvious to me that one should prototype and allow people to 
play their game a lot more to get a grip of how well suited the mechanics are, 
but now I have a better picture.

> Grab assets from online, figure out the mechanics and netcode, let people play!
