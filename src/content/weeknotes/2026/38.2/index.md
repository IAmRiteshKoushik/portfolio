# Tech

This is a second weeknote coming out this week. I don't usually do this but I 
thought the details are worth sharing. So, this is the week I picked up 
agentic development seriously (especially in the last few days) and also the 
time my frustrations with it grew 10-fold.

Going agentic is much less about the model and a lot about what your harness is 
good at and what it isn't and figuring out solutions to those shortcomings. While 
the model plays a major role, you can still get really good performance out of 
a cheaper model if you can steer it well.

This weekend, one of my major mistakes in my previously `undisclosed` project 
was that I got through the planning sessions really well but I did not setup 
the entire stack of skills to handle the codebase implementation parts. This 
meant that while my planning covered all edge cases and I was able to run 
parallel agents to pick up tasks, it became a sloppy-mess that I had to cleanup 
by hand once the bigger implementation parts came in.

> Most of my weekend went off in making these fixes and then I kinda gave up for 
a good while to restore morale!

Now, my twitter feed is blowing up with Jev and it's endless possibilities but 
also major hit-pieces on [cloudflare](https://opensauce.it/cloudflare-ai-psychosis/)
which I cannot fully disagree with. And not to mention, as always lots of 
pretty UIs and micro-animations everywhere.
