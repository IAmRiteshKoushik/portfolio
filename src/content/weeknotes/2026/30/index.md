This week has been fairly interesting in terms of work, personal growth and 
overall health improvements. Few key incidents have course-corrected major 
portions of my life. It would not be an understatement to say that last week 
was anything but eventful and things were getting derailed.

So, let me list out the events and how they contributed to fixes

1. Went to watch Christopher Nolan's - The Odessey this week after work hours 
with a bunch of colleagues. A pretty interesting movie although not one of 
Nolan's best works. The movie took the entire first half to set the tone but 
the ending made it worth it. Although I still don't feel that a runtime of 
close to 3 hours is justified and the plot was weak, but the visuals, the 
cinematography and the sound design wrapped it up pretty well. 

2. Earlier the week, I cruised through 3 seasons of Ted Lasso on Amazon Prime. 
I can see why the series appeals to the larger audience. It had its ups and 
downs. The humanizing part of Ted where he suffers through breakdowns, eventually 
accepts vulnerability and had a positive attitude all form a pretty cocktail. 
Although there are instances where the character goes from optimistic to 
borderline panglossian which becomes irritating but its worth bearing through 
for the themes tackled within the show.

3. Work was a bit more interesting this week too. I have been added to a team 
which handles critical entry flow into HDFC as well as some backoffice operations. 
Going forward I would be working at both MCP layer as well as with this team. 
Finally got access to Jira and Confluence (sigh of relief!). GitHub access is 
still hanging in the balance but I have acquired the codebases and documentation 
so I can spend the weekend reading through the flows and familiarizing myself. 
I have also picked up a very interesting way of using `interfaces` in Go and 
figured out how code generation works under the hood in tools like `sqlc` as 
it became a part of my job.

4. Beginning of the week, I wrapped up reading Project Hail Mary (the book) and 
it sort of reignited my interest in math and science. I was looking through 
some of the codebases of NASA's Jet Propulsion Lab.

5. Now, as luck would have it, I decided to speak to Revanth later in the week 
after a long hiatus and he dumped a ton of stuff on my head. Combinatorial 
problems, optimization problems via linear programming and what not! He's on 
a roll. Honestly, very very motivating for me to pick up the pace and get my 
shit together too. 

6. My diet has improved. Much less food from outside. Although a slight obsession 
with avocado milkshakes and mango milkshakes has been creeping up. I need to 
nip it otherwise it would bloat into full-blown diabetes. My exercise equipment 
has arrived which means I can start working out the way I want to from this 
week onwards. This is a good sign. It is also a signal to finally fix my 
sleep schedule which is already a work in progress.

7. Finally, I have started sketching again. It seems like I am doing better 
with the pens that with shading pencils although a lot of practice would still 
have to be done with pencils. I would like to finally get a grip on human 
figures, fabrics, animals and different objects.

That's the week in a nutshell. Looking forward to a promising week ahead.
